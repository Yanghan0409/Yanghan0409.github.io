I am a Postdoctoral Fellow in the Department of Chemistry at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST). My research focuses on the design and biomedical application of aggregation-induced emission (AIE) luminogens for cancer phototheranostics, with particular interest in lipid droplet (LD)-targeting AIEgens for type I/II photodynamic therapy (PDT) and their integration with tumor immunomodulation (natural killer cells, dendritic cells, and M1 macrophages) to enhance antitumor immunotherapy.

My broader interests include AIE-based nanotheranostics for photothermal and photodynamic therapy, brain-disease nanotheranostics (encephalitis, neurodegenerative diseases, and brain tumors), ferroptosis regulation and neuroprotection, and antibacterial phototherapy.
